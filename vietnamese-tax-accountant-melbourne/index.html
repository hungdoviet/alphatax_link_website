Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our Vietnamese tax accounting services in Melbourne.

Why is hiring a Vietnamese-speaking tax accountant beneficial in Melbourne? ➕

Australian tax legislation is notoriously intricate. A Vietnamese-speaking CPA removes all linguistic confusion, ensuring you clearly understand your ATO obligations, allowable business deductions, capital gains implications, and entity tax structures without misinterpretation.

Where is your office located in Melbourne? ➕

Our office is located at 105/29A Leeds Street, Footscray VIC 3011, directly in the heart of Footscray. We welcome in-person appointments as well as seamless remote consultations via phone, email, and video conference across Greater Melbourne and Australia.

Can you assist with business setup for new Vietnamese migrants and visa holders (188/888)? ➕

Yes. We specialize in business establishment, ABN/ACN registration, GST setup, trust structures, Section 52 vendor statements for business purchases, and financial reporting aligned with Australian immigration visa compliance.

How do you help if I have overdue or late tax returns with the ATO? ➕

As Registered Tax Agents, we can access your full ATO history, prepare and lodge multi-year overdue individual or company returns, and submit formal requests for ATO general interest charge (GIC) and failure-to-lodge penalty remissions.

Do you handle BAS, GST, and payroll for Vietnamese-owned businesses? ➕

Yes, we manage quarterly and monthly BAS lodgements, GST reconciliations, Single Touch Payroll (STP Phase 2), Superannuation Guarantee compliance, and bookkeeping integration on Xero and MYOB for restaurants, retail, trades, and professional services.

How can I book a consultation with Tien Nguyen FCPA? ➕

You can contact our office by phone at (03) 9689 3908 or 0422 312 758, email info@alphataxlink.com.au, or fill out the consultation form on our website. We offer consultations in both English and Tiếng Việt.
